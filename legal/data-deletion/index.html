Effective Date: August 10, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 10, 2026

1. Your Right to Deletion

Under data protection laws including the GDPR (EU/UK) and CCPA (California), you have the right to request deletion of your personal data. This page explains what data can be deleted, how to submit a request, and what to expect.

For full details on what data we collect and how we handle it, see our Privacy Policy.

3. How to Request Data Deletion

Because this is a self-hosted single-user deployment, there are two ways to delete your data:

Method 1: Self-Service Deletion If you still have access to your account, log in at psa.innotel.us and navigate to your Account Settings. Use the account deletion option to remove your account and associated data directly.
Method 2: Email Request If you cannot access your account, send a deletion request to admin@innotel.us with:
  • Subject line: "DATA DELETION REQUEST"
  • The email address associated with your account
  • Any additional identifying information to help us locate your data

4. What Happens After You Submit a Request

  1. Acknowledgment (within 48 hours): We will confirm receipt of your request by email.
  2. Verification (1–3 days): We verify your identity to ensure we're deleting the right data. For email requests, we may ask for additional confirmation.
  3. Deletion (within 30 days): We will delete all deletable personal data within 30 calendar days of verified request, as required by GDPR.
  4. Confirmation: We will notify you once deletion is complete, specifying what was removed and any data retained per legal requirements.

Billing records retained for legal compliance are stored in a restricted-access format and used only for tax, audit, or legal defense purposes. They are not used for marketing or analytics.

6. Third-Party Data

Deleting your account with us removes your data from our systems, but it does not automatically remove content already published to social media platforms (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Slack, Mastodon, Threads, Dribbble, GitHub, and Beehiiv). To remove published posts, you must:

  1. Log in to each social media platform directly
  2. Manually delete the posts you published through Postiz
  3. Follow each platform's own data deletion process if you wish to delete your platform account

You may also want to revoke Postiz's OAuth access from each platform's app settings before deleting your account. This ensures no residual access remains. Instructions for common platforms:

  • X/Twitter: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Other applications
  • Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites
  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions
